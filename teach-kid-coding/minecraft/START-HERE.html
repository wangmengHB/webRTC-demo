Tips for parents

Keep sessions to 20–30 minutes. Let your child type the code themselves, even slowly — ownership matters! When errors appear, read them together as puzzles to solve. Name variables after his favourite things (steveX, creeperSpeed). Celebrate every mission with a screenshot. All 6 files work offline — no internet needed after opening!
