Two Loops = Full Grid!
🔲

One loop fills a row. A loop inside a loop fills the whole grid! The outer loop goes down (rows), the inner loop goes across (columns).

This is called a nested loop — one of the most powerful patterns in game programming!

🧠 Think about it! 8 columns × 8 rows = 64 blocks drawn with just a few lines of code!
