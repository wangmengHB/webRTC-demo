Meet the Canvas
🎨

In Minecraft, the world is made of blocks on a grid. On a computer screen, everything is also a grid — millions of tiny dots called pixels.

We draw on something called a canvas — it's like a blank piece of graph paper where we can color in squares!

The canvas has a position system: x goes left→right, y goes top→bottom. The top-left corner is (0, 0).

🌍 Minecraft fact! A Minecraft chunk is 16×16 blocks — just like a grid. We'll build our own grid soon!
